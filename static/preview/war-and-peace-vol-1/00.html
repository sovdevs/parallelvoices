How to read this edition

Each paragraph is followed by its other-language version.

Words with a dotted underline and a small number are explained with the sentence pair they come from, the original and its translation.

Character names are highlighted in colour wherever they appear, in both languages. Characters without a colour of their own share grey. Tap or hover a name to see who it is.
